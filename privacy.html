Privacy Policy

ShotPerfect · Last updated: February 2026

1. Information We Collect

ShotPerfect is designed with privacy in mind:

  • No personal information: We don't collect your name, email, or any identifying information.
  • No tracking: We don't use analytics or tracking tools.
  • No image uploads: Your screenshots are processed entirely on your device.

2. Photos Access

ShotPerfect requests access to your photo library to import screenshots and save your finished designs. Your photos never leave your device.

3. iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync, your projects are stored in your personal iCloud account. This data is encrypted by Apple and not accessible by us.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive confirmation of your purchase but no payment details.

5. Third-Party Services

  • RevenueCat: For managing in-app purchases. See their privacy policy.
  • Apple CloudKit: For iCloud sync, governed by Apple's privacy policy.

6. Children's Privacy

ShotPerfect does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.
