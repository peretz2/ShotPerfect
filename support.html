FAQ

What export sizes are supported?

iPhone 6.9" (1320×2868), 6.7" (1290×2796), 6.5" (1284×2778), 5.5" (1242×2208), and iPad 13" (2064×2752), 12.9" (2048×2732).

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to Settings → Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID as the original purchase.

Why is there a watermark?

Free version exports include a watermark. Upgrade to Premium ($6.99 lifetime) to remove it.

How do I sync across devices?

Sign into the same Apple ID on both devices with iCloud Drive enabled for ShotPerfect.

Can I get a refund?

Contact Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com

System Requirements

iOS 17.0 or later. Works on iPhone.
