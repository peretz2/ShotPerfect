Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a new project?

Tap the + button on the main screen and select your target device sizes.

How do I add a screenshot?

Open a project and tap "Add Screenshot" to import from your photo library.

How do I export my screenshots?

Tap the export button (top right) and choose "Save to Photos" or "Save to Files".

What sizes are supported?

ShotPerfect supports all required iPhone screenshot sizes: 6.9", 6.7", 6.5", and 5.5".

What's included in Premium?

Premium unlocks unlimited projects, all templates, clean exports (no app branding), and all device frame colors. It's a one-time purchase.

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to Settings and tap "Restore Purchases".

System Requirements

iOS 17.0 or later. Works on iPhone.
